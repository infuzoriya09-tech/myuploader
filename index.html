Publish short vertical videos to TikTok from your desktop

MyUploader is a lightweight Windows desktop app for creators who edit Shorts-style videos on their computer. Sign in with TikTok, pick a finished video, review the caption and privacy settings, and post it — or send it to your TikTok inbox as a draft.

How it works

  1. Sign in with TikTok. MyUploader opens TikTok's official authorization page in your browser. You choose what to allow; the app only receives the permissions you approve.
  2. Choose a video. Pick a finished MP4 from your video folder and watch the preview before anything is sent.
  3. Review the post. The app shows which TikTok account you are posting to, lets you write the caption, choose who can view the video (no default — you decide), and turn comments, Duet and Stitch on or off. You can disclose promotional or branded content.
  4. Post or save as draft. "Post to TikTok" publishes the video directly. "Send to TikTok inbox" uploads it as a draft so you can add sounds and effects in the TikTok app and publish from there.
  5. See the result. The app shows the upload and processing status until TikTok confirms the post or delivers the draft.

Your data stays on your computer

MyUploader has no servers and no accounts of its own. Your TikTok access token is stored only in a local file on your computer and is used only to talk to TikTok's official API. Videos go straight from your computer to TikTok. Signing out deletes the token. Read the Privacy Policy.
